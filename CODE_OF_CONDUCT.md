# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. By participating you agree to uphold it.

## In short

- Be respectful, welcoming and patient. Assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **[CONDUCT CONTACT EMAIL]**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review, investigate and respond in line with the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
